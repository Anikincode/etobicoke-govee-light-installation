The short answer

What is Govee light installation?

Govee light installation is a hired service where a crew mounts your Govee permanent outdoor lights along your roofline for you. That means planning where the run starts and ends, fastening every clip to the fascia, hiding the cables, and connecting it to an outdoor GFCI outlet. You keep the lights and the app. We handle the ladder.

Below: what we light, photos of our installs, why a "DIY" kit is harder than the video makes it look, what's included, what to measure before you buy, and answers to the questions people ask first.

Why hire it out

Why hire out a kit that's sold as DIY?

Because the hard part isn't the lights. It's everything around them. Even Govee's own support page says installing at roofline height "requires caution" and recommends a helper.

The height

The peak of a two-storey house is high. A wet or icy ladder in late fall is how a Saturday project ends in the emergency room.

The fit

The Permanent Outdoor Lights 2 can't be cut, so a wrong start leaves extra lights with nowhere to go. The Pro, in runs up to 200 ft, has to be cut to fit, and a cut in the wrong place can't be undone.

The weather

The clips come with adhesive tape. Tape holds best on a clean, dry, warm surface, and a Toronto fascia in November is usually none of those.

The power

The kit plugs into an outdoor GFCI outlet. If yours is at the back and the run starts at the front, the cable has to get there neatly.

Made for your roofline

What's included when we install your Govee lights?

Every house is different: the peaks, the soffits, the garage, and where the outdoor outlet is. Here's what the install covers, from the first look at your roofline to the first scene in the app.

Tell us about your house

  1. We walk the roofline first

    Fascia, drip edge, corners, peaks and where your outdoor outlet is. Then we plan where the run starts, where the joins land and where the controller sits.

  2. We cut Pro runs to fit

    Govee's Permanent Outdoor Lights Pro comes in runs up to 200 ft, and installing it properly means cutting it to your roofline. We cut it where the run needs to end and connect it the way Govee's manual says.

  3. We mount the clips properly

    Into the fascia or under the drip edge, following the lines of the roof. Nothing goes through your shingles.

  4. We tuck the cables

    Power and connector cables follow the trim instead of hanging across the wall.

  5. We shelter the power box

    The light strings are rated IP67. On the Permanent Outdoor Lights 2, the power adapter and control box are only IP65, so they don't go where the eavestrough drips. On the Pro, Govee lists the adapter for indoor use.

  6. We test every section

    You shouldn't be the one who finds a dead run on the first cold night.

  7. We set up the app with you

    Lights connected, a few scenes saved and a quick walkthrough so you can change them yourself. If your Wi-Fi doesn't reach the front of the house, you'll find out now, not three days later.

Before you buy

Haven't bought your kit yet? Measure first.

This is the most common mistake, and it happens before anyone touches a ladder. Govee sells its permanent lights in set lengths, and the two main kits work differently. Buy a Permanent Outdoor Lights 2 too long and you can't trim the extra. Buy a Pro and it gets cut to fit, up to 200 ft.

  • Measure every roofline you actually want lit, not just the front.
  • Count the peaks. A front gable uses far more length than a flat run along the eaves.
  • Note where your outdoor outlet is, and how far it is from where you want the lights to start.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are Govee permanent outdoor lights?

A run of weatherproof LED lights that mounts once along your roofline and stays up all year. You control colour, brightness and schedules from the Govee app, so the same lights work for every night, Halloween, Christmas and game night.

Can you see them during the day?

They tuck under the roof edge, so from the street they're easy to miss in daylight. Stand in the driveway and look up and you'll see them. Anyone who says they're invisible is overselling.

Do I need an outdoor outlet?

Yes. Govee's instructions say to "install only to a covered Class A GFCI receptacle": an outdoor outlet with ground-fault protection and a weatherproof cover. If you don't have one near where the lights will start, adding one is a job for a licensed electrician. We install the lights. We don't do electrical work.

Can the lights be cut to fit my house?

It depends on the model. Govee says the Permanent Outdoor Lights 2 can't be cut, so the run is planned around the length you buy. The Permanent Outdoor Lights Pro can be cut and comes in runs up to 200 ft, and to install it properly, we cut it to fit your roofline. Our length planner works out which lengths fit.
