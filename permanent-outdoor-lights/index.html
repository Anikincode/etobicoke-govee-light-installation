Permanent Outdoor Lights in Etobicoke

Every November, someone climbs a ladder with a box of tangled lights. Every January, they climb it again to take them down. Permanent lights go up once and stay.

Permanent outdoor lights are LED lights mounted once along your roofline that stay up all year. You control them from an app, so the same lights are warm white on a Tuesday, orange for Halloween and red and green for Christmas. We install Govee's permanent outdoor lights on homes across Etobicoke.

Permanent Christmas lights

Are permanent lights the same as permanent Christmas lights?

Yes. "Permanent Christmas lights" is what a lot of people call them, because Christmas is when most people first want them. The real difference from regular Christmas lights is what happens the other eleven months.

QuestionRegular Christmas lightsPermanent outdoor lights
Goes upEvery NovemberOnce
Comes downEvery JanuaryIt doesn't
ColoursWhatever the string came withAny colour, changed in the app
The rest of the yearIn a box in the garageWarm white every night, or off
Ladder tripsTwo a year, at leastThe install, and that's on us

So the question isn't really "Christmas lights or permanent lights". It's whether you want to do the ladder every year, or never.

Before the install

What does your house need before permanent lights go up?

Not much, but these four things decide how smooth the job is.

  • A roofline you want lit. The eaves, the peaks, the garage. Measure what you can from the ground, and use our length planner to add it up.
  • An outdoor outlet. For the Permanent Outdoor Lights 2, Govee says to use a covered Class A GFCI outlet. If you don't have one near where the run starts, a licensed electrician adds it. We don't do electrical work.
  • Trim in decent shape. The clips mount along the roof edge, so loose or damaged trim is worth fixing first.
  • Wi-Fi at the front of the house. The lights connect over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, so check your signal reaches where the control box will sit.

Cost

How much do permanent outdoor lights cost?

There are two parts: the lights and the install.

The lights. Govee sells its kits directly, and its prices change often, so check Govee's site for the current price of the length you need. A longer roofline means a longer kit or extension lights. If you'd rather not buy it yourself, we can supply the kit.

The install. It depends on how much roofline you want lit, how many peaks and corners there are, and how high the house is. Every house gets its own quote: send us the details and we'll tell you what yours costs.
