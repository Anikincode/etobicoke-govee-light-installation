Christmas Light Installation in Etobicoke

Want the house lit for Christmas without spending a cold Saturday on a ladder? That's the part we do.

We put Christmas lights up on Etobicoke homes before the holidays and take them down in the new year. Prefer lights that never come down? We also install Govee permanent outdoor lights that go red and green every December and warm white the rest of the year. Every house gets its own quote.

Below: how a seasonal install works, how it compares with permanent lights, what Christmas looks like on our installs, and when to book.

Seasonal installs

How does seasonal Christmas light installation work?

Two visits. One before the holidays to put the lights up, and one in the new year to take them down. You don't climb anything either time.

  1. Send us the details

    Your street, what you'd like lit (roofline, peaks, garage) and whether you already have lights. A photo from the street helps.

  2. Up before the holidays

    We put the lights up where you want them and plug them into your outdoor outlet.

  3. Down in the new year

    We come back after the holidays and take them down, so you're not on the roof in January.

No outdoor outlet near the roofline? Adding one is a job for a licensed electrician. We install the lights. We don't do electrical work.

Seasonal vs permanent

Seasonal or permanent: which suits your house?

Seasonal lights are a December service. Permanent lights are a one-time install you use every month after.

QuestionSeasonal Christmas lightsPermanent Christmas lights
When they go upBefore the holidays, every yearOnce, any time of year
When they come downA second visit in the new yearThey don't
ColoursWhatever strings were hungRed and green, multicolour or warm white, changed in the app
StorageBoxes of lights in the garageNothing to store
The rest of the yearA bare rooflineWarm white every night, or off

We do both. Seasonal suits you if you only want lights in December. Permanent suits you if you're tired of the yearly up and down, or want the house lit for Halloween and every evening too. More on permanent outdoor lights.

Christmas scenes

What does Christmas look like with permanent lights?

Whatever you'd have hung, plus a few things strings can't do.

  • Classic warm white. The look of traditional white Christmas lights, along every roofline.
  • Red and green. Alternating along the run, or one colour on the house and one on the garage.
  • Multicolour. Like the strings you grew up with, in one clean run along the roofline.
  • A schedule. On at dusk and off at bedtime, from late November to New Year's, without anyone touching a phone.

Timing

When should I book a Christmas light install?

As early as you can.

For seasonal lights, book before the rush. In Canada, searches for Christmas light installation peak in November, so that's when most people try to book.

For permanent lights, earlier still. The clips use adhesive tape, and tape holds best on a clean, dry, warm surface. By December, a Toronto fascia is rarely any of those.

Going permanent

Which Govee kit works for Christmas lights?

Both of Govee's main kits do every Christmas colour. The biggest difference between them is cutting. You can't cut the Permanent Outdoor Lights 2, so the run is planned around the length you buy. You can cut the Permanent Outdoor Lights Pro, which comes in runs up to 200 ft, and to install it properly, we cut it to fit your roofline.
